### Case Study 001: The Scripted Academic Simulation (CASE-001_HARVARD_SIM)

* **Context:** A highly viral video capturing sharp geopolitical or legal debates inside what appears to be a real Harvard lecture hall. The script aligns perfectly with textbook international law concepts.
* **Text-Only Vulnerability:** The baseline LLM reads the transcript, recognizes the complex structural arguments, and concludes that this is an organic, historic academic event. It cannot see the actors. It cannot verify the environment. It falls victim to a text-level smokescreen.
* **SFL Detection Vectors:** 

  * **Environmental Audio Phase:** The audio stream exhibits uniform digital noise instead of spatial dampening typical of an authentic, crowded lecture hall.
  * **Micro-gesture Discontinuity:** The SFL frame analyzer flags a 12% drift in physiological micro-movements relative to continuous human neural motor responses, revealing a meticulously staged and rehearsed performance.
